SC Codeworks in the News

SC Codeworks and its warehouse management platform, Codeworks Enterprise, have been recognized by leading industry publications and award programs. Below you will find our latest press coverage, awards, and product announcements.

Awards & Recognition

SupplyTech Breakthrough Awards — Warehouse Management System of the Year (2025)

SC Codeworks was named the "Warehouse Management System of the Year" at the 2025 SupplyTech Breakthrough Awards. This program recognizes the top companies, technologies, and products in the global supply chain technology market. The award highlights Codeworks Enterprise's comprehensive capabilities for 3PL, distribution, and manufacturing warehouse operations.

Inbound Logistics — Top 100 Logistics IT Providers

SC Codeworks has been selected as one of Inbound Logistics' Top 100 Logistics IT Providers, a prestigious list that recognizes the leading technology companies serving the logistics and supply chain industry. This recognition reflects the depth of Codeworks Enterprise's capabilities and its impact on warehouse operations across North America.

Supply Chain Brain — 100 Great Supply Chain Organizations

SC Codeworks was named to Supply Chain Brain's 100 Great Supply Chain Organizations list, which highlights companies that are making significant contributions to supply chain excellence. This recognition underscores SC Codeworks' role as a technology partner that helps warehouses and 3PLs operate more efficiently.

Manufacturing Technology Insights — Top WMS 2023

Manufacturing Technology Insights recognized Codeworks as a Top WMS solution in 2023, citing the platform's flexible architecture, robust feature set, and ability to serve both manufacturing and distribution warehouse environments.

Product Announcements

2025

Codeworks Enterprise 4.0 Launch

The release of Codeworks Enterprise 4.0 represents the most significant update to the SC Codeworks warehouse management platform. Version 4.0 introduces AI-powered operational insights, enhanced dashboard reporting, expanded automation capabilities, and new integration connectors for leading ERP and e-commerce platforms. The release reinforces SC Codeworks' commitment to delivering a WMS that drives measurable operational improvements for 3PLs, manufacturers, and distributors.

June 2025

CODI AI Assistant Launch

SC Codeworks announced the launch of CODI, an AI-powered assistant built directly into the Codeworks Enterprise platform. CODI provides warehouse operators with intelligent recommendations, natural language queries for warehouse data, predictive analytics for volume planning, and automated anomaly detection. CODI represents the next generation of warehouse management technology, bringing AI directly into the hands of operations teams.

March 2025

Development Advisory Board Established

SC Codeworks announced the formation of its Development Advisory Board, a group of industry professionals and current customers who provide strategic guidance on product development priorities. The Advisory Board ensures that Codeworks Enterprise continues to evolve based on the real-world needs of warehouse operators, 3PL providers, and supply chain leaders. Read more about the Advisory Board.
