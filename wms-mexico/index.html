Warehouse Management System for Mexico Operations

Sistema de Gestión de Almacenes para Operaciones en México

As nearshoring accelerates and Mexico cements its position as the top trading partner of the United States, warehouse and distribution operations across the country are scaling at an unprecedented pace. SC Codeworks delivers Codeworks Enterprise, a bilingual warehouse management system purpose-built for the complexities of cross-border commerce between Mexico and the US. Whether you operate a single distribution center in Monterrey or manage a network of fulfillment facilities spanning the Bajío corridor, Codeworks Enterprise gives your team real-time inventory visibility, automated workflows, and the compliance tools required to keep goods moving across borders without delays.

Our WMS is trusted by third-party logistics providers, manufacturers, and distributors who need a system that works seamlessly in both English and Spanish, integrates with the ERP platforms already running your back office, and scales as your Mexico operations grow. Nuestro WMS es utilizado por proveedores de logística, fabricantes y distribuidores que necesitan un sistema que funcione en inglés y español.

Why Mexico Needs a Modern WMS

The Nearshoring Boom

Mexico has emerged as one of the most strategically important locations for warehousing and manufacturing in the Western Hemisphere. The nearshoring trend, driven by companies seeking to shorten supply chains and reduce dependence on distant overseas production, has brought a wave of new warehouse construction and logistics investment across Mexico. According to industry reports, industrial real estate vacancy rates in key Mexican cities have fallen below 2%, and new warehouse space is being absorbed almost as fast as it is built.

Cities like Monterrey, Guadalajara, Querétaro, San Luis Potosí, and the broader Bajío region have become thriving manufacturing and distribution corridors. These areas host operations for global automotive companies, consumer electronics manufacturers, aerospace suppliers, and consumer packaged goods firms. Every one of these operations depends on efficient warehouse management to keep production lines running and orders shipping on time.

Cross-Border Supply Chain Complexity

Operating warehouses in Mexico introduces layers of complexity that domestic-only operations never encounter. Cross-border inventory management requires tracking goods as they move between Mexican and US facilities, managing customs documentation, handling duties and tariffs, and maintaining compliance with trade regulations under USMCA (the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement). A warehouse management system that was designed only for domestic US operations will struggle with these requirements.

The right WMS must handle multiple currencies, support bilingual documentation, manage bonded warehouse requirements, and integrate with customs brokers and freight forwarders who facilitate cross-border movements. Codeworks Enterprise was built with these exact challenges in mind, providing a unified platform that treats your Mexico and US warehouses as a single, connected network.

The Need for Bilingual Systems

Warehouse floor teams in Mexico operate primarily in Spanish. Management and corporate stakeholders in the US operate in English. A modern WMS for Mexico must bridge this gap seamlessly. This is not simply a matter of translating menu labels. It means Spanish-language pick tickets, receiving documents, packing slips, shipping labels, and mobile device interfaces for floor workers, combined with English-language dashboards, reports, and analytics for US-based management. Codeworks Enterprise delivers this bilingual capability natively, not as an afterthought or a bolt-on translation pack.

Codeworks Enterprise for Mexico

Codeworks Enterprise is a full-featured warehouse management system that has been deployed in complex, multi-site operations across North America. For Mexico operations specifically, it offers the following capabilities:

Bilingual Interface (English/Spanish)

Every screen, report, label, and notification in Codeworks Enterprise can be displayed in English or Spanish. Users set their preferred language at login, and the system adapts accordingly. This means your warehouse associates in Guadalajara see instructions in Spanish on their RF scanners and mobile devices, while your operations manager in Dallas reviews the same data on English-language dashboards. Bilingual support extends to customer-facing documents, so your clients receive communications in the language they prefer.

Cross-Border Inventory Management

Codeworks Enterprise provides unified inventory visibility across all your facilities, whether they are in Mexico, the United States, or both. The system manages inter-facility transfer orders, tracks in-transit inventory crossing the border, and maintains lot-level and serial-number traceability that customs authorities may require. You can view real-time stock levels at every location from a single dashboard, allocate inventory from the optimal facility based on customer proximity, and plan replenishment across borders with full visibility into lead times and transit requirements.

Mexican Customs and Trade Compliance Support

Operating in Mexico means complying with SAT (Servicio de Administración Tributaria) requirements, managing IMMEX program documentation for temporary imports, and maintaining records that satisfy both Mexican and US customs authorities. Codeworks Enterprise supports the documentation workflows required for bonded warehouses, maquiladora operations, and free trade zone facilities. The system generates the data needed for Pedimentos, Cartas Porte, and other Mexican trade documents, and integrates with customs broker systems to streamline border crossings.

Multi-Site Management Across Mexico and US

Many companies operating in Mexico manage warehouses on both sides of the border. Codeworks Enterprise provides true multi-site management from a single platform. Each warehouse can have its own workflows, picking strategies, and operational rules while sharing a unified inventory database and reporting framework. This means your team in Querétaro and your team in San Antonio are working from the same data, with the same accuracy, in real time.

EDI and ERP Integration

Codeworks Enterprise integrates with all the major ERP platforms used by companies operating in Mexico, including SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, NetSuite, and Epicor. The system supports standard EDI transactions (856, 940, 945, 944, 943, and more) and offers REST API connectivity for modern integration patterns. Whether your ERP runs in Mexico City or in your US headquarters, Codeworks Enterprise connects to it reliably and keeps data synchronized across systems.

Industries We Serve in Mexico

Automotive Manufacturing

Mexico is the seventh-largest automobile manufacturer in the world and the largest in Latin America. Automotive supply chains in Mexico are extraordinarily complex, with just-in-time delivery requirements, sequence-specific shipping to assembly plants, and strict quality traceability standards. Codeworks Enterprise supports automotive warehousing with kitting and assembly workflows, VIN-level tracking, ASN (Advanced Shipping Notice) generation, and integration with OEM portals. Our system helps automotive suppliers in the Monterrey-Saltillo corridor, the Bajío region, and Puebla manage their warehouse operations with the precision these supply chains demand.

Consumer Goods Distribution

Mexico's growing consumer market has attracted major CPG companies and retailers who need efficient distribution networks to serve 130 million consumers. Codeworks Enterprise handles high-volume, high-SKU distribution operations with wave planning, zone picking, cartonization, and automated shipping integration. Whether you are distributing to Walmart de México, OXXO, Chedraui, or direct-to-consumer, our WMS optimizes throughput and accuracy.

E-Commerce Fulfillment

E-commerce in Mexico has grown rapidly, with platforms like Mercado Libre, Amazon Mexico, and direct-to-consumer brands driving demand for fast, accurate fulfillment. Codeworks Enterprise supports e-commerce fulfillment with batch picking, pack-and-ship workflows, multi-carrier shipping integration, and real-time order status updates. The system handles the high order volumes and tight delivery windows that e-commerce customers expect.

Food & Beverage

Mexico's food and beverage industry requires warehouse management systems that support temperature-controlled storage, lot tracking with expiration date management (FEFO picking), COFEPRIS compliance, and detailed recall traceability. Codeworks Enterprise provides all of these capabilities, along with catch-weight processing and allergen tracking for operations that handle perishable and regulated food products.

Pharmaceutical

Pharmaceutical warehousing in Mexico must comply with COFEPRIS regulations, maintain strict chain-of-custody documentation, and support serialization requirements. Codeworks Enterprise offers the lot-level traceability, controlled-substance management, and regulatory documentation features that pharmaceutical distributors and manufacturers need to operate compliantly in the Mexican market.
